About LA Live Stream

Your one-stop-shop for all live streaming and event production needs.

LA Live Stream opened in 2010 to fulfill the demand for professional production studios in Los Angeles. In our 15+ year history, we have grown into a high-tech live streaming production house that serves mixed media clients from Los Angeles and the Bay Area, but also worldwide.

We are an amazing team of creative & professional live streaming production technicians and broadcasters who are dedicated to producing elite quality live video services that help businesses & corporations engage their consumers, employees & business partners in today's networked world.

As a renowned events specialist, we promise to take care of all your needs to ensure that you have a reliable and stress-free experience. Leave it up to us to take care of all your technical needs, stress-free!
